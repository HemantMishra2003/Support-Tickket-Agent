# PW Payment, Batch Access and Refund Support

## 1. Purpose

This document handles student queries related to:

- Payment failures
- Pending transactions
- Duplicate payment deductions
- Successful payments with missing batch access
- Purchased batches not appearing in student accounts
- Course access problems
- Refund requests
- Batch change requests
- Damaged or lost books

## 2. Common Issues

Students may report the following problems:

- Payment failed during batch purchase.
- Money was deducted, but the transaction is pending.
- Money was deducted, but the payment status shows failed.
- Money was deducted twice for the same purchase.
- Payment was successful, but the purchased batch is unavailable.
- A purchased batch is missing from the student's account.
- The student cannot access course lectures.
- The student wants a refund.
- The student purchased the wrong batch and wants to change it.
- Books were damaged or lost during courier delivery.
- Books could not be delivered because of an incorrect address.
- The student refused to accept the delivered books.

## 3. Payment Support

### 3.1 Payment Failed

When a student reports a failed payment:

- Ask the student to check the transaction status in their
  bank or payment application.
- Do not claim that the payment failed permanently without
  transaction verification.
- Do not tell the student to make another payment immediately
  without understanding the current transaction status.

### 3.2 Money Deducted but Transaction Pending

When money has been deducted but the transaction is pending:

- Explain that the transaction status may still be pending.
- Ask the student to check the latest transaction status
  with their bank or payment provider.
- Do not guarantee that the money will be refunded.
- Do not invent a specific transaction settlement timeline.

### 3.3 Money Deducted but Payment Shows Failed

When the student reports a deduction but the payment status
shows failed:

- Ask the student to verify the transaction status with
  their bank or payment provider.
- Collect the transaction reference number, if available.
- Do not claim that the money has been reversed without
  confirmation.
- Escalate the issue for human review if it remains unresolved.

### 3.4 Duplicate Payment Deduction

When a student reports being charged twice:

- Ask for the relevant transaction reference numbers.
- Collect the payment date and amount.
- Do not claim that a duplicate payment has been confirmed
  without transaction verification.
- Escalate unresolved duplicate deductions for human review.

### 3.5 Payment Successful but Batch Unavailable

When a student reports successful payment but cannot access
the purchased batch:

- Ask for the batch name and Batch ID, if available.
- Collect the transaction reference number, if available.
- Do not claim that payment or enrollment has been verified.
- Flag the issue for human review if batch access remains
  unavailable.

## 4. Batch Access Support

### 4.1 Common Batch Access Problems

Students may report:

- Purchased batch is not visible in their account.
- Student cannot access purchased course content.
- Batch access is missing after payment.
- Student is unable to open course lectures.
- Student has paid but cannot access the purchased batch.

### 4.2 Information to Collect

When a student reports a batch access problem, collect:

1. Student Name
2. Registered Phone Number
3. Batch Name
4. Batch ID, if available
5. Transaction Reference Number, if relevant
6. Brief Description of the Problem

### 4.3 Batch Access Handling Rules

- Available verified sources do not provide detailed
  troubleshooting steps for batch access.
- Do not invent troubleshooting instructions.
- Do not claim that payment or enrollment has been verified.
- Do not claim that batch access has been restored.
- Do not promise a specific resolution time without
  verified information.
- Flag unresolved batch access problems for human review.

## 5. Refund Policy

### 5.1 General Refund Policy

When a student purchases PW products or services, the
purchase is not entitled to a refund.

Online batches and services are non-refundable.

Do not promise a refund for an online batch or service.

### 5.2 Batch Change Policy

If a student purchases a batch by mistake, they can request
to change it to another batch of the same amount within
10 days of the purchase.

Handling rules:

- Explain the batch change option when the student has
  purchased the wrong batch.
- The requested replacement batch must be of the same amount.
- The request must be made within 10 days of purchase.
- Do not claim that the batch change has been completed
  without confirmation.
- Do not guarantee approval or completion beyond what
  the stated policy supports.

### 5.3 Books and Physical Products

Students are advised to check the complete system before
making a payment for books.

#### Damaged or Lost Products

If the product received is damaged or lost by the courier
partner, the student is entitled to a replacement.

Do not promise a cash refund when the stated policy provides
for a replacement.

#### Incorrect Address

If the student provides an incorrect address, the purchase
is not entitled to a refund.

#### Student Refuses Delivery

If the student refuses to accept the books, the purchase
is not entitled to a refund.

### 5.4 Refund Handling Rules

- Do not promise a refund for an online batch or service.
- For a mistaken batch purchase, explain the option to
  request a change to another batch of the same amount
  within 10 days of purchase.
- For books damaged or lost by the courier partner,
  explain the replacement policy.
- Do not promise a refund for books when an incorrect
  address was provided or the student refused delivery.
- If the student's situation is not covered by the available
  policy, flag it for human review.
- Do not claim that a refund, replacement, or batch change
  has been completed without confirmation.

## 6. Information to Collect

Collect only the information relevant to the student's issue.

Possible details include:

- Student Name
- Registered Phone Number
- Batch Name
- Batch ID, if available
- Transaction Reference Number, if relevant
- Payment Date
- Payment Amount
- Payment Status
- Brief Description of the Issue

Never ask students to share:

- Passwords
- OTPs
- UPI PINs
- Banking PINs
- Complete card details

## 7. Escalation Rules

Flag the issue for human review when:

- A transaction requires verification.
- A duplicate deduction remains unresolved.
- Payment status cannot be established.
- Payment succeeded but batch access remains unavailable.
- Account or enrollment verification is required.
- A refund eligibility decision cannot be determined from
  the available policy.
- The student's situation is not covered by this document.
- The available knowledge base cannot resolve the issue.
- The student explicitly requests human assistance.

Flagging an issue for human review does not mean that the
issue has already been forwarded to or resolved by a human
support team.

## 9. Student Satisfaction and Follow-up Rules

### 9.1 Ask for Satisfaction

After providing an answer to a student's payment,
batch access, or refund query, ask:

"Are you satisfied with this response?"

### 9.2 If the Student Is Satisfied

If the student responds with "Yes", "Satisfied", or
otherwise clearly confirms that their concern has
been addressed:

- Mark the conversation as resolved.
- Do not escalate the issue unnecessarily.
- Do not claim that a payment, refund, or batch change
  has been completed unless it has been verified.

### 9.3 If the Student Is Not Satisfied

If the student responds with "No", "Not satisfied",
"Still facing the issue", or indicates that the answer
did not resolve their concern:

- Acknowledge the student's concern politely.
- Ask one relevant follow-up question if additional
  information could help clarify the issue.
- If the issue still cannot be resolved, flag it for
  human review.
- If the student explicitly requests human assistance,
  flag the issue for human review immediately.

### 9.4 Follow-up Response Template

I'm sorry that your concern hasn't been resolved.

Could you please share a little more information about
the issue so I can better understand your concern?

### 9.5 Human Review Response Template

I'm sorry that we haven't been able to resolve your concern.

I'll flag this issue for human review so that it can
be reviewed by the appropriate support team.

Please note that flagging an issue does not mean it
has already been forwarded to or resolved by the team.

### 9.6 Important Rules

- Do not repeatedly ask the student the same question.
- Do not repeat the same answer without addressing the
  student's concern.
- Do not keep the student in an endless conversation loop.
- Never override the verified refund policy because the
  student is dissatisfied.
- If the issue requires transaction verification, account
  verification, or a decision outside the available policy,
  flag it for human review.
