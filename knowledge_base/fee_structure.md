# Physics Wallah Fee Structure Support

## 1. Purpose

This document provides information about Physics Wallah (PW)
course fees for NEET, JEE, and Foundation programs.

It helps the AI assistant answer student queries about
online course fees, offline registration fees, and
available course offers.

## 2. NEET Coaching Fees

### NEET Online

- Starting price: ₹2,999.

### NEET Offline

- Registration fee: ₹5,000.
- This amount is the seat booking price.

The offline registration fee may vary depending on the
course, class level, and city.

## 3. JEE Coaching Fees

### JEE Offline

The offline registration fee may vary depending on the
course, class level, and city.

## 4. Foundation Course Fees

## 5. Offline Registration Fee Details

According to the available PW Vidyapeeth fee structure:

### NEET Batches

- Class 11 NEET: ₹5,000 registration fee.
- Class 12 NEET: ₹5,000 registration fee.
- Dropper NEET: ₹5,000 registration fee.

### JEE Batches

- Class 11 JEE: ₹5,000 registration fee.
- Class 12 JEE: ₹5,000 registration fee.
- Dropper JEE: ₹5,000 registration fee.

### Foundation Batches

- Class 8 Foundation: ₹5,000 registration fee.
- Class 9 Foundation: ₹5,000 registration fee.
- Class 10 Foundation: ₹5,000 registration fee.

These amounts are registration fees, not necessarily
the total course fees.

## 6. Available Course Offers

According to the provided fee structure document:

- JEE courses for Classes 11–12 and droppers:
  Discounts of up to 35%.
- NEET courses for Classes 11–12 and droppers:
  Discounts of up to 35%.
- Foundation courses for Classes 8–10:
  Discounts of up to 40%.

Offers and discounts may vary by course and eligibility.
Do not guarantee a discount without verifying the
applicable offer.

## 8. AI Response Rules

- Clearly distinguish online starting prices from offline
  registration fees.
- Do not describe the ₹5,000 registration fee as the
  complete offline course fee.
- Explain that online prices are starting prices and may
  vary by batch.
- Explain that offline fees may vary by course, class,
  and city.
- Do not invent exact fees when they are unavailable.
- Do not guarantee discounts without verifying eligibility.
- If the exact fee is not available, ask the student for
  the course, class, and city.
- If the available information cannot answer the query,
  flag it for human review.
